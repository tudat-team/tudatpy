Desai, S. D. and Sibois, A. E. (2016), Evaluating predicted diurnal and semidiurnal tidal variations in polar motion
with GPS-based observations, J. Geophys. Res. Solid Earth, 121, 5237-5256, doi:10.1002/2016JB013125 (supporting
information data set ds01). 159 ocean-tide terms, columns as in the IERS 2010 Tables 8.2/8.3 files of this directory:
  *PolarMotion*Amplitudes: x sin, x cos, y sin, y cos [microarcseconds]
  *Ut1*Amplitudes: UT1 sin, UT1 cos [microseconds]
  *FundamentalArgumentMultipliers: gamma(=GMST+pi), l, l', F, D, Omega
The published LOD amplitudes are not used: UT1 rates follow from the UT1 series.
