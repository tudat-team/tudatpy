# Juno spacecraft range data
#
# Range measurements to Juno spacecraft.
#
# These are subject to improvement as the gravity field estimate improves with time
# and the spacecraft trajectories are subsequently improved.
#
# Current as of 15 Oct 2019
#
#
# Description of columns;
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
#    Bytes Format  Units   Label    Explanations
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
#    1-  3  I3     ---     nnn      Spacecraft identifying number (061 for Juno)
#    5-  6  I2     ---     up       DSN transmitting station ID
#    8-  9  I2     ---     dn       DSN receiving station ID
#   11- 14  I4     ---     yyyy     Year of measurement time (UTC)
#   16- 18  I3     ---     mm       Month of measurement time (UTC)
#   20- 21  I2     ---     dd       Day of measurement time (UTC)
#   23- 24  I2     h       hh       Hour of measurement time (UTC)
#   26- 27  I2     min     min      Minute of measurement time (UTC)
#   29- 30  I2     sec     sec      Second of measurement time (UTC)
#   32- 48  F17.10 sec     rtlt     Round trip light time
#   53- 58  F5.2   us      Ddelay   Delay between arrival of the spacecraft signal
#                                   at the station and the time of the measurement, in microseconds.
#   61- 62  I2     ---     pl       Planet number (5 for Jupiter)
#   64- 80  F17.6  s       t2       TDB time of spacecraft position and velocity
#                                   in second past the TDB epoch J2000.0
#   82- 99  F18.6  km      x        X coordinate of Juno spacecraft with
#                                      respect to Jupiter System Barycenter
#  101-117  F18.6  km      y        Y coordinate of Juno spacecraft with
#                                      respect to Jupiter System Barycenter
#  119-136  F18.6  km      z        Z coordinate of Juno spacecraft with
#                                      respect to Jupiter System Barycenter
#  139-149  F11.6  km/s    vx       X velocity of Juno spacecraft with
#                                      respect to Jupiter System Barycenter
#  151-161  F11.6  km/s    vy       Y velocity of Juno spacecraft with
#                                      respect to Jupiter System Barycenter
#  163-173  F11.6  km/s    vz       Z velocity of Juno spacecraft with
#                                      respect to Jupiter System Barycenter
# --------------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
